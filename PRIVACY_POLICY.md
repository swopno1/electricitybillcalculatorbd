## 1. Introduction

ViveScript Solutions LLC ("we", "us", or "our") develops focused, privacy-conscious utility applications. This Privacy Policy describes how **Electricity Bill Calculator BD** (the "Application") handles user information.

We believe in maximum privacy: **The core calculation functionality of Electricity Bill Calculator BD operates completely offline, without requiring an account, registration, or transmission of personal data.**

---

## 2. Information We Do NOT Collect

We do NOT collect, transmit, store, or sell any of the following:

- Personal identification information (name, email address, phone number, physical address)
- Government IDs or utility account credentials
- Electricity consumer meter numbers or utility bill images
- Precise or approximate GPS location data
- Contacts, address books, SMS, or call logs
- Biometric data or camera/microphone inputs

---

## 3. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

The Application stores a minimal set of preferences **strictly on your local device** using Android's private `SharedPreferences`:

1. **User Interface Preferences:** Selected language (Bangla or English), display theme (System, Light, Dark), and demand charge toggle state.
2. **Recent Calculation History:** Up to the last five (5) calculated usage records (units, date, and estimated bill amount) to enable convenient recall.

*Note:* This information never leaves your device and can be cleared at any time by tapping "Clear" in the History section or by clearing the app's storage in Android Settings.

The core bill calculation operates completely offline and does not access the internet. The Application includes the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to display non-intrusive banner advertisements and policy-compliant interstitial placements:

- **AdMob Integration:** Configured with ViveScript Solutions LLC's Google AdMob identifiers (App ID: `ca-app-pub-5222053984568989~6515064111`, Banner Unit ID: `ca-app-pub-5222053984568989/6167579759`, Interstitial Unit ID: `ca-app-pub-5222053984568989/9212634784`).
- **Data Handled by Google Play Services / AdMob:** When connected to the internet, Google Mobile Ads may process general device identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID), coarse diagnostic data, and ad interaction metrics to deliver and measure ad performance in compliance with Google Play Developer Policies. Ad frequency is strictly capped to protect user experience.

For more information regarding how Google processes data, please visit:  
[Google Privacy & Terms](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [Google Ads Technologies](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads).

## 5. Third-Party Services & Links

## 6. Permissions

The Application requests only standard, normal install-time permissions:
- `android.permission.INTERNET` (Required for displaying AdMob advertisements)
- `android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE` (Required to verify network status for ad requests)

The Application requests **zero sensitive or runtime-protected permissions** (no location, no camera, no storage access).
